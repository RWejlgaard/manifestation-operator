By default every namespace except the control-plane ones is subject to manifestation.

1. Create something in any namespace. Watch it sit Pending / SchedulingGated:
     kubectl -n <your-namespace> create deployment nginx --image=nginx
     kubectl -n <your-namespace> get pods

2. Speak your Desire into being (present tense, as if already true):
     cat <<EOF | kubectl apply -f -
     apiVersion: manifestation.pez.sh/v1alpha1
     kind: Desire
     metadata:
       name: nginx-is-serving
       namespace: <your-namespace>
     spec:
       manifestation: "My nginx pod is healthy and serving traffic"
     EOF

   The pod schedules. You did that. With your mind.
